The standard

Don't compete on price.
Compete on finish.

A cheaper quote usually means less prep. Here's what you're paying for with us — and why it holds up.

  1. 01
    Prep is the job. Most paint failures start underneath. We wash, scrape, sand, fill and prime before any topcoat goes on.
  2. 02
    Made for the Bay. Salt air, strong UV and humid summers are hard on paint. We spec systems that are built for coastal conditions.
  3. 03
    Clean lines. Clean site. Floors and furniture covered, gear packed away each evening. You'll live in your home while we work.
  4. 04
    Clear quote. No surprises. A fixed, itemised price before we start. If something changes on site, you hear about it first.

FAQ

Questions people ask before they call

Can't see yours? Give us a ring or send a message — we'll get back to you within one working day.

How much does it cost to paint a house?

It depends on size, condition and how much prep the surfaces need. We price every job after a free on-site visit, and you get a fixed, itemised quote — not a ballpark.

How long will the job take?

Most interiors take a few days; a full exterior repaint usually runs one to three weeks depending on size and weather. We give you a timeframe with the quote.

Do I need to move out or move furniture?

No. We move and cover furniture, protect floors, and work room by room so you can keep living at home.

What paint do you use?

[Your preferred brands and systems] — chosen for the surface and for Bay of Plenty conditions. Happy to talk through options at the quote visit.

Can you help choose colours?

Yes. Bring your ideas and we will bring swatches. We can also suggest combinations that suit your home and its light.

Which areas do you cover?

The wider Bay of Plenty. Get in touch with your suburb and we will confirm.
